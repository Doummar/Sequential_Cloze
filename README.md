# Sequential Cloze

A focused way to review cloze cards in Anki.
Reveal information step by step instead of all at once.

## Review Modes

- **Sequential Reveal:** Reveal hidden parts one by one on the same card.
- **Sequential Context:** Review ordered items with nearby context using `{{c1::}}`, `{{c2::}}`, `{{c3::}}`...

## How to review

- Press **Space** or **Enter** to reveal the next cloze
- Click **[...]** to reveal or hide that cloze
- Press **Shift + Space** to reveal all clozes at once
- Mouse wheel down can also reveal the next cloze
- When all clozes are revealed, press **Space** to flip the card

## Sequential Context options

- **Context Before:** previous items shown on the front
- **Context After:** next items shown on the front
- **Back Context Before / After:** how much context to show on the back
- **Mask Subsequent Items:** hide future items outside the context window

## Shortcuts

- **G** → toggle Image
- **H** → toggle Info
- You can change shortcuts and mouse bindings in **Settings → Reveal Controls**

## Optional fields

- **Visible Image** → always shown on the card
- **Image** → hidden until you press G (or the Image button)
- **Info** → hidden until you press H (or the Info button)
- **Front Audio / Back Audio** → play buttons in the control bar
- **Explanation** → shown on the back of the card

## Settings

- **General:** layout, font family, font size, bold cloze text
- **Reveal Controls:** reveal speed, click-to-reveal, shortcuts
- **Compatibility:** optional custom cloze colors
- **Sequential Context:** enable mode and context options

## Notes

- Settings are preserved across updates
- Cards remain usable on AnkiWeb and mobile
- Styles are scoped so other note types are not affected
